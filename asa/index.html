What this is

We searched for MeAgain the way a new GLP-1 user does, then rebuilt what happens after the tap.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. For MeAgain we looked at two things. First, your app: a recording of your iOS onboarding from first launch to the paywall (26 steps, 13 questions), your App Store listing and meagain.com. Second, the App Store searches that lead to it: your brand, category, drug-name and competitor keyword groups, and how much of each you already win.

What follows is the five ideas from our MeAgain Growth Audit, and two keyword flows that show what happens when each search gets its own version. Every one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and all of it is clickable. The prototype uses your design, not ours.
